### Why OpenCV was chosen for GompeiVision

I selected OpenCV for the image processing.

- **Platform Independence**: Since one of my goals was to support a wide variety of hardware, I needed a library that could handle camera capture and image processing without being tied to a specific platform.
- **Robust Algorithms**: OpenCV has excellent cross-platform support and is used heavily in both robotics and research.
- **Focused Scope**: Using OpenCV allowed me to focus on solving the localization problem rather than writing low-level image processing code from scratch.
