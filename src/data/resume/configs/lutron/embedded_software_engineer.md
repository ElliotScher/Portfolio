# Embedded Software Engineer — Lutron Electronics

On-site 5 days/week

## Overview

As an Embedded Software Engineer at Lutron, you will contribute to high-stakes projects from the start, helping to maintain Lutron's position as the global leader in smart lighting and shading systems.

Working within a collaborative, cross-functional team, you'll help bring innovative consumer products to life by tackling real engineering challenges using skills in various hardware interfaces, distributed cloud services, and a variety of wireless technologies.

You'll grow through direct mentorship from industry experts, building hands-on experience across the full product lifecycle with a clear path forward deepening technical expertise while gaining leadership skills through your projects and development as an integral part of the team.

You will encounter a variety of technologies throughout the product lifecycle, including:

- Smart IoT systems
- Distributed cloud services
- Various hardware interfaces, subsystems and wireless technologies
- Contemporary software development tools and languages
- Embedded Linux

Your contributions will utilize the following foundational areas:

- Design and development of algorithms and data structures
- Object oriented analysis and design
- Real-time operating systems
- Protocol design and architecture
- Agile software methodology

## Responsibilities

- Eliciting software requirements from various stakeholders.
- Participating in software design and implementation.
- Testing, deploying, managing, and maintaining software components.
- Participating in consensus-based software project management.
- Prioritizing feature estimating.
- Collaborating in reviews and demos.

## Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or Computer Science.
- Minimum, cumulative GPA of 3.00 for each degree earned.
- 0-3 years' experience in a related field.
- Authorized to work in the U.S. without visa sponsorship, now or in the future.
- Willingness and ability to work on-site 5 days per week.

## Compensation

$98,500–$104,500/yr plus bonus. Benefits include 401k match, health/dental/life insurance, paid vacation, flexible spending accounts, and tuition reimbursement.
