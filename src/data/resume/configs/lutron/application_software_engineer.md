# Application Software Engineer — Lutron Electronics

On-site 5 days/week

## Overview

As an Application Software Engineer, you will make an impact from day one, helping to maintain Lutron's market position as the leader in smart lighting and shading systems. At Lutron, your code doesn't sit in a backlog — it ends up in millions of homes and buildings around the world.

Working within a collaborative, cross-functional team, you'll help bring innovative consumer products to life by tackling real engineering challenges across algorithms and data structures, distributed cloud services, and user experience optimization, ensuring software quality through rigorous testing.

You'll grow through direct mentorship from industry experts, building hands-on experience across the full product lifecycle with a clear path forward deepening technical expertise while gaining leadership skills.

You will encounter a variety of technologies throughout the product lifecycle, including:

- Smart IoT systems
- Distributed cloud services
- Desktop, web, and mobile applications
- Contemporary software development tools and languages

Your contributions will utilize the following foundational areas:

- Efficient design and use of algorithms and data structures
- Object oriented analysis and design
- Service oriented architecture
- Information retrieval, data management and persistence
- Software debugging, testing and tracing
- Agile software methodology
- User experience optimization

## Responsibilities

- Eliciting software requirements from various stakeholders.
- Participating in software design and implementation.
- Testing, deploying, managing, and maintaining software components.
- Participating in consensus-based software project management.
- Estimating planned implementation and prioritization.
- Reviewing software designs and requirements before demoing to stakeholders.

## Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or Software Engineering.
- Minimum, cumulative GPA of 3.00 for each degree earned.
- 0-3 years' experience in a related field.
- Authorized to work in the U.S. without visa sponsorship, now or in the future.
- Willingness and ability to work on-site 5 days per week.

## Compensation

$98,500–$104,500/yr plus bonus. Benefits include 401k match, health/dental/life insurance, paid vacation, flexible spending accounts, and tuition reimbursement.
