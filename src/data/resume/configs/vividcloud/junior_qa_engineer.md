# Junior QA Engineer — Robotics, Physical AI & AI — VividCloud

Entry Level / Recent Graduate

## Overview

VividCloud is looking for a talented and curious recent graduate who wants to build a career in Robotics, Physical AI, and Artificial Intelligence.

This is an entry-level QA Engineering position, but we see QA as the starting point—not the destination. You will begin by working alongside our engineering team to test real-world software systems, understand how complex applications behave, identify problems, and help automate testing. As you develop your skills and demonstrate your abilities, our goal is to expand your involvement into our Robotics and Physical AI initiatives.

## What You'll Do

- Test software, APIs, applications, and integrated systems.
- Find, reproduce, document, and help troubleshoot software defects.
- Learn and contribute to automated testing using tools such as Python/PyTest, Postman, and modern testing frameworks.
- Work directly with software, cloud, DevOps, AI, and robotics engineers.
- Develop a strong understanding of how complex software systems interact with real-world environments.
- Grow into increasingly technical work involving AI, simulation, robotics, and Physical AI.

## Who We're Looking For

We're more interested in potential, curiosity, and technical ability than years of experience.

You may be a recent graduate in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, Robotics, Electrical/Mechanical Engineering, AI, or a related field.

You should enjoy solving problems, figuring out how things work, and learning new technologies. Exposure to Python, Git, APIs, Linux, automated testing, robotics, simulation, ROS/ROS2, NVIDIA Isaac Sim, machine learning, or AI is a plus—but we don't expect a recent graduate to know all of them.

If you want your first engineering role to become a pathway into Robotics, Physical AI, and AI, we'd like to talk with you.
