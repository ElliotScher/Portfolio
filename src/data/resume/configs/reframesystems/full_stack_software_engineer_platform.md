# Full Stack Software Engineer - Platform Software — Reframe Systems

Onsite · Andover, MA (30 Lowell Junction Road, 01810)

## About Us

Reframe Systems is a Physical AI and robotics company on a mission to make high-performance housing attainable and abundant in the communities where people want to live. Through vertically integrated delivery and highly automated microfactories, we deliver climate-resilient homes with greater speed and predictability than traditional construction. We produce volumetric modular buildings in Andover, MA, with expansion planned across North America, and our cross-disciplinary team integrates architecture, robotics, design engineering, software, factory operations, and field delivery.

## What We're Looking For

A Senior Software Engineer to reimagine how construction projects are managed from inception through build. Today, information gets re-entered across sales, estimating, procurement, and factory operations — you'll own how a project flows through our systems, from the first customer conversation through to what the factory builds, designing one authoritative representation of a project so every downstream function works from a single source of truth. You'll also integrate our design automation platform with factory software to close feedback loops between planning and actual production.

## Key Responsibilities

- Own how a project flows through our systems, from the first customer conversation through to what the factory builds.
- Design the underlying data models and system of record so all functions work from one authoritative project representation.
- Build full-stack applications that convert manual processes into operational software.
- Define data contracts and interfaces between business functions.
- Apply AI to unstructured components of our workflows where it delivers real production value.
- Partner directly with the teams who will use what you build to find the highest-leverage problem, and close feedback loops between planning and actual production.
- Set technical direction for how the system evolves, and mentor other engineers.
