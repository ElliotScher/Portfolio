# Robotic Systems Integration Engineer (SLA & SLS) — June 2027 Grads — Formlabs

Hardware Engineering · Somerville, MA

## About Formlabs

Formlabs builds the tools that make it possible for anyone to bring their ideas to life, from cutting-edge products to life-saving medical devices. Its 3D printing technology powers innovation at more than 50,000 industry leaders worldwide, including Apple, Google, Tesla, New Balance, and NASA.

## Your Impact

Formlabs has a substantial business selling 3D printers and resins to dental customers, who use its products for applications ranging from in-office splint production to sophisticated lab-based orthodontics. This role supports the full product development cycle, from early R&D to continuing engineering on shipped products, collaborating closely with design teams to solve cross-disciplinary problems and ship quality products.

## The Job

- Bring together the firmware and hardware of subsystems into one system, carry prototypes through integration, and ensure they work optimally against requirements to deliver physical prototypes.
- Partner with the system architect to create and document visual descriptions of systems and subsystems and their inputs and outputs.
- Establish interfaces between subsystems, define/refine control algorithms, and design electro-mechanical subsystems.
- Own management of subsystem/system level requirements and track changes.
- Design test plans to validate subsystem/system requirements, execute the tests, and analyze results.
- Resolve systems issues to improve performance or recommend corrective actions to the functional group.

## About You

- Previous experience designing complex electro-mechanical systems.
- Knowledge of motion control, sensors, and control algorithms.
- Experience managing system and subsystem requirements.
- Well-versed in hardware debugging and passionate about understanding why things work (or don't).
- Scripting experience with Python (preferred), LabVIEW, MATLAB, etc.

## Bonus Skills

- Familiarity with low-level embedded firmware (C++ preferred).
- Experience in data analysis and reporting.
- Experience with consumer electronics.
