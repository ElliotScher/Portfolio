# Full Stack Engineer I — CherishHealth

## Overview

We seek a Full Stack Engineer I to help build the software platform behind our smart sensing device. In this role, you will work across our entire technology stack — including our privacy-first edge platform, secure backend services, and polished user experiences on consumer-facing mobile applications and enterprise web applications — learning to deliver reliable alerts, real-time data, and workflow management at scale.

## What You'll Do

- Contribute to a multidisciplinary team responsible for the design, development, and maintenance of our end-to-end software platform.
- Build and maintain backend services for device management, system events, and communication workflows.
- Develop intuitive features for our web and mobile applications.
- Explore and implement event-driven and real-time patterns, including message queues, WebSockets, and push notifications, to ensure seamless data flow across the ecosystem.
- Learn the architectural principles behind API versioning, backward compatibility, and auditability.
- Build your instincts for safety-oriented workflows and agent-driven capabilities in a controlled, testable environment.

## Cross-Functional Collaboration

Your work will span the full software lifecycle — collaborating closely with senior engineers, product managers, designers, and hardware teams to implement robust, scalable, and secure features. While the role focuses primarily on software and feature development, you will also collaborate with electronics, supply chain, and program management teams in a supportive, safe, and highly collaborative team setting to achieve our broader company goals.
