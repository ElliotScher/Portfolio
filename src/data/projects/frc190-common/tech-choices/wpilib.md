### WPILib for FRC 190 Codebases

WPILib is the official suite of software libraries used for the FIRST Robotics Competition, acting as the foundation for all robot operations:

* **Command-Based Architecture**: Structures the codebase around modular, asynchronous commands (like joystick drive or targeting), enabling clean composition of complex parallel actions.
* **Kinematics & Odometry**: Includes math libraries for driving swerve, differential, or mechanum drivetrains, enabling precise field-relative positioning.
* **Simulation Framework**: Supports offline robot physics simulation to validate autonomous routines and joystick control without physical hardware.
