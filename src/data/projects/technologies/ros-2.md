### ROS 2 Robotics Middleware

I utilize ROS 2 (Robot Operating System) to develop distributed, modular robotic software architectures. I design publisher/subscriber nodes, configure hardware interfaces, and integrate sensor nodes (LIDAR, cameras) for navigation pipelines.

#### Project Portfolio:
- **[Robotic Navigation](#project-RBE3002)** — Maze mapping, SLAM, and frontier exploration implemented as standard ROS 2 nodes.
- **[Generic Robotic Systems Framework](#project-RBE300X)** — A custom pub-sub architecture inspired directly by the ROS 2 computational graph.
